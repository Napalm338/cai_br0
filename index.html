Help

Speech
Pick a speech to load. Switching speeches downloads any unsaved log first.
CAI toggle
On: popups show synced text cues and log every render's timing. Off: plain audio, no popups, nothing logged. Works mid-playback.
Play / Pause / Stop / Progress bar / Volume
Play/Pause starts or pauses playback. Stop halts and resets to the start. The progress bar shows and sets position. The volume slider sets volume.
Download CSV
Downloads the current speech's log (render time, marker text, audio time, latency) as CSV. Enabled once something's been logged.
